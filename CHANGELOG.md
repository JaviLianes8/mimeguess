## [0.1.0]

- `guess_type` maps a path to a MIME type from its extension.
- `guess_extension` maps a MIME type to its preferred extension.
- Vendored table covering text, web, images, audio, video, archives, office
  documents, fonts and common binaries.
- Case-insensitive lookups, OS-independent results, zero dependencies.
